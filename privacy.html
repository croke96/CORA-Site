CORA

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Summary

  • Your CORA data stays on your own devices and on a sync service you run yourself.
  • CORA uses Google data only to provide the features you use in CORA.
  • CORA does not sell your data, show ads, or share it with data brokers.
  • You can disconnect Google at any time, and delete CORA's copy of your data.

Who is responsible

CORA is a personal, non-commercial project. Questions about this policy can be sent to keegan.ds.cross@gmail.com.

Information CORA stores

CORA stores the information you create in it (notes, lists, reminders, projects, learning material, conversations with the assistant, and memories the assistant keeps about your preferences and context) in a local database on your device. If you use several devices, they exchange this information through a sync service that you host and control. There is no central CORA server.

Google user data

If you choose to connect a Google account, CORA requests access only to the services you approve on Google's consent screen:

  • Gmail: to show, search and organise your mail in CORA, and to prepare and send messages when you ask. CORA asks you to confirm before it sends an email.
  • Google Calendar: to show your calendars and events, and to create or change events when you ask.
  • Google Contacts: to show and update the people you work with.
  • Google Drive: to find, read and edit files you work on with CORA.
  • Your email address: to show which Google account is connected.

How it is used. Google data is used only to provide and improve the CORA features you use. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, and it is not used to train general-purpose AI models.

Where it is kept. Copies that CORA keeps to work offline are stored on your own devices. Sign-in credentials are stored in your operating system's protected credential storage or in files readable only by your user account.

AI processing. CORA's assistant is powered by Anthropic's Claude, used through the CORA user's own Claude account. When you ask the assistant to work with your information, the relevant content is sent to Claude to produce the answer or carry out the task. That processing is governed by Anthropic's terms and privacy settings for your Claude account.

Limited Use. CORA's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other services

CORA may contact other services you choose to use, for example a weather service for local forecasts, a private chat service you configure for remote access, or optional voice providers. Only the information needed for that feature is sent.

Sharing

CORA does not sell, rent or trade your information. It shares information only with the services described above, only to provide the features you use, or where required by law.

Your choices

  • Disconnect Google in CORA's settings, or remove CORA from your Google Account permissions.
  • Edit or delete memories and other records in CORA at any time.
  • Uninstall CORA and delete its local data folder to remove its copy of your information from a device.

Security

CORA keeps sign-in credentials in protected storage, signs and verifies its updates, and authenticates every device that joins your sync service. No system is perfectly secure, but CORA is designed so that your information stays under your control.

Children

CORA is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
