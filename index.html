CORA

A private, local-first personal assistant for Windows.

What CORA is

CORA is a desktop assistant that helps its user manage notes, lists, reminders, projects, learning, calendar, mail and files in one place. It can answer questions, remember useful context, and carry out tasks the user asks for.

CORA is a personal project. It is not sold, and it has no accounts, subscriptions or advertising.

Local-first by design

Your CORA data is stored on your own devices. If you use more than one device, CORA keeps them in step through a sync service that you run yourself. There is no central CORA server holding your data.

Connecting Google

When you choose "Sign in with Google", CORA asks for access to the Google services you want it to work with: Calendar, Gmail, Contacts and Drive. You can grant some and not others, and you can remove CORA's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

How CORA handles that information is described in the Privacy Policy.
